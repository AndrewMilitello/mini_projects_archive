# Mini Projects Archive

This repository contains a collection of mini projects I created over the past few years while teaching myself Python and developing my understanding of artificial intelligence and computer science.
The projects cover a range of topics that I explored at different points in my learning, varying significantly in age, scope, and completeness. Some are early experiments from when I was first learning to program, while others reflect more recent interests. I have kept them together as an archive of my earlier work and as a record of the different areas I explored while learning.
## Projects

**Algorithms** — Algorithmic and computational projects

**Games** — Games and interactive projects

**ML** — simple Machine learning models implementations

**NLP / AI** — Natural language processing, AI projects, or language-related applications

**Procedural** — Procedural generation and related algorithms

**Systems** — Systems-oriented projects and simulations

## About the Archive

These projects are not intended to represent my current level of work. Rather, they document my progression as I learned programming and explored different areas of computer science and artificial intelligence.

I have kept the projects in their original form where practical, with only limited cleanup, so that the repository remains a record of the work I completed while learning.
